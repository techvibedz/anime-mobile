# Pantoufa — Arabic launch advertisement

## Approved direction

A cinematic, approximately 35–40 second advertisement introducing Pantoufa to
Arabic-speaking anime fans. The user selected a warm, confident male narrator in
Modern Standard Arabic. Produce a 1080 × 1920 social master and a separately
composed 1920 × 1080 landscape master, both with Arabic captions.

Measured narration and comfortable reading holds produce a final runtime of
45 seconds. Preserve the natural Arabic delivery rather than accelerating it.

## Creative treatment

Use the current P/play logo, Cairo Arabic type, Outfit Latin type, and the actual
`lib/theme.ts` palette: near-black, periwinkle, and restrained mint. The existing
anime poster assets carry the visual color. Floating device compositions,
frame-driven camera moves, staggered typography, and purposeful feature
animations supply the motion graphics. Use stylized screen recreations derived
from the current app source, with fictional demonstration data.

## Story

1. Hook: every story deserves to be experienced; cinematic anime poster orbit.
2. Introduction: Pantoufa puts the anime world in your hands; home screen reveal.
3. Discovery: favorite titles and new episodes in one place; animated catalogue.
4. Continuity: resume and keep favorites; playback and saved-position animation.
5. Offline: download episodes and watch later without internet; download-to-ready.
6. Social: synchronized viewing with friends; connected devices and shared play.
7. Conversion: Pantoufa, anime your way; Android CTA and a scannable release link.

## Audio

Generate Arabic narration with a native Arabic neural voice. Scene lengths follow
measured audio durations, including comfortable transition and reading holds.
Create an original low-key electronic score and transition sound design. Keep
narration forward in the mix, with no clipping and a natural fade-out.

## Acceptance

- Finished MP4s, editable Remotion source, Arabic script/subtitles, and a thumbnail.
- Correct connected Arabic glyphs and RTL layout, with no cropped headlines.
- A complete audio track and readable captions throughout the spoken sections.
- Exact current brand assets; verified download, resume, and watch-party features.
- CTA links to `https://github.com/techvibedz/anime-mobile/releases/latest`.
- Visual contact sheets inspected before full-resolution rendering; final media
  checked for duration, dimensions, audio presence, decodability, and loudness.
